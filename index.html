Getting Started Takes Four Steps

How Pickup and Delivery Works

Sign Up for Pickup and Delivery
  1. 1

    Schedule Your Pickup

    Schedule online, through our app, by phone, or by text. Call 864-583-8668 anytime during store hours, and we'll confirm service at your address.

  2. 2

    Confirm Your Pickup Details

    We'll go over your first pickup with you, including how to prepare your items and where to leave them. Pickups run Monday through Saturday, with your service days set by your area and route.

  3. 3

    Let Us Handle the Cleaning

    We clean your items as requested. Share any garment concerns or special requests when you schedule your order.

  4. 4

    Receive Your Clothing Back

    We bring your clean clothing back to your door, usually within 2 to 3 business days. Timing depends on the service, the garment, any stains, and your location, and specialty items may need a little longer.

Answers Before Your First Order

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a charge for pickup and delivery?

Yes. Pickup and delivery carry a service charge. Call 864-583-8668 for current rates and to confirm service at your address.

Do you offer same-day service?

Yes. Call 864-583-8668 before bringing your items in to confirm the deadline and availability for your order.

Can you help with regular laundry?

Yes. Our Laundry and Wash and Fold service takes care of your regular washing, drying, and folding, and our Shirt Laundry & Professional Pressing service keeps your shirts crisp and ready to wear.

Do you offer alterations?

Yes. Our Alterations & Tailoring service includes garment adjustments and repairs. Contact us about your item and any fitting arrangements.

Where can I drop off my cleaning?

Bring your order to 448 Marion Ave, Spartanburg, SC 29306. We're open Monday through Friday from 7:30 a.m. to 6 p.m. and Saturday from 8 a.m. to noon.
