Choose the Service You Need

Shirt Laundry or Wash and Fold?

If you want your shirts laundered and professionally pressed, this is the service for you. For everyday laundry that just needs washing, drying, and folding, choose our Laundry & Wash and Fold service.

Some shirts have care labels that call for a different approach. Bring in the label and any questions, and we'll point you to the right service. Bell Laundry & Cleaners also offers dry cleaning and expert wet cleaning.

Before Your Next Shirt Order

Shirt Laundry & Professional Pressing FAQs

Does shirt laundry include professional pressing?

Yes. Every shirt is laundered and professionally pressed, then returned folded or on hangers.

Is this different from wash and fold?

Yes. Wash and fold covers washing, drying, and folding. Choose Shirt Laundry & Professional Pressing when you want your shirts pressed.

Can I send shirts with special care instructions?

Yes. Share the instructions with our team when you drop off, and we'll recommend the right service for each shirt.

How long will my shirts take?

Ask about timing when you drop off. Pickup and delivery orders typically come back within 2 to 3 business days, depending on the order and your location.

How do I get pricing for shirt laundry?

Call 864-583-8668 or stop by our Marion Avenue store. Let us know how many shirts you have and any special care concerns, and we'll give you current pricing.
