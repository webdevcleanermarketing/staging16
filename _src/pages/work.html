Good Service Starts With People

Behind Every Order Is Someone Counting on Us

Behind every order is someone counting on us. It might be a shirt for the workweek, fresh linens for home, or a dress for an important occasion. The care we put into each item matters to the person who brings it in.

Bell Laundry & Cleaners is a family-owned business established in Spartanburg in 1941. Today, our team carries that tradition forward with quality workmanship, helpful service, and lasting customer relationships. If you take pride in doing a job well, we'd love to hear from you.

Care in the Details. Respect for the Customer.

Our work calls for attention, reliability, and a willingness to help. We value people who handle customers' belongings thoughtfully, communicate clearly, and understand that small details can make a lasting impression.
