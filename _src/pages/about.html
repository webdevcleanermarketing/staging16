Who We Are

Family-Owned Since 1941

Choosing a cleaner means trusting someone with the clothes you wear for work, everyday life, and important occasions. At Bell Laundry & Cleaners, we take that responsibility seriously.

Bell Laundry & Cleaners is a family-owned business, established in Spartanburg in 1941. We combine professional garment care with a focus on quality workmanship, convenience, and personal service. Our customers include busy professionals, families, healthcare and hospitality workers, and local businesses across the Upstate and select Western North Carolina communities.

A History Worth Carrying Forward

More Than Eight Decades of Garment Care

Bell Laundry and Cleaners was founded in 1941 with a simple commitment: dependable garment care for the Spartanburg community. Over the decades, we've carried that tradition forward while adapting to modern cleaning technology, equipment, and the changing needs of our customers.

We are a Sanitone Certified Master Dry Cleaner and also offer expert wet cleaning, so your garments get the care that suits them. Our experienced staff takes pride in handling your clothing with care.

What We're Here to Do

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide exceptional dry cleaning and laundry services with quality, care, and dependable customer service. That means treating every garment with attention to detail, using modern cleaning methods, and making garment care convenient through reliable pickup and delivery.

Here's what that means when you visit or call. You'll get a team that listens to what you need, clear answers and pricing before you leave your order, and follow-up when you need it. Reach us by phone or email, or stop by our Marion Avenue store.
