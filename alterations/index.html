A Fit That Feels Like You

Alterations & Tailoring in Spartanburg, SC

There's nothing like putting on something that fits just right. When a hem is too long, a jacket feels snug, or a seam gives way, Bell Laundry & Cleaners can help with clothing alterations and tailoring in Spartanburg, SC.

Call About Alterations

From hemming and sleeve shortening to repairs, bring us the pieces you'd like adjusted and tell us what you have in mind.

Make More of Your Wardrobe

Small Adjustments Can Make a Big Difference

Trousers that drag at the heel or a jacket that pulls across the shoulders can sit in the closet for months. A few alterations can bring those pieces back into your rotation before you think about replacing them.

Maybe you're updating an outfit for work, getting ready for a celebration, or bringing back a much-loved piece you haven't worn in a while. Tell us what you love about it and what you'd like to change, and we'll take it from there.

Alterations for Everyday and Occasion Wear

Explore Our Alterations & Tailoring Services

01

Hemming and Sleeve Shortening

Get the right length with hemming and sleeve shortening, including pants hems.

02

Suit, Blazer, and Tuxedo Alterations

We resize suits, adjust jacket sleeves, and taper trousers, including women's suits and blazers. Tuxedo hemming and tapering are also available.

03

Dress Restyling and Resizing

Give a dress or gown you already own a new fit or a fresh shape with restyling and resizing.

04

Bridal and Bridesmaid Alterations

We alter wedding dresses and bridesmaid dresses. Bring in your gown and share your wedding date early, so there's plenty of time to plan the work.

05

Denim Alterations

Jeans and corduroys hemmed, tapered, or adjusted at the waist.

06

Overcoat and Leather Alterations

Overcoat resizing, relining, length adjustments, and inner-pocket replacement, along with leather alterations such as jacket lining replacement.

07

Clothing Repairs

Tear repairs and replacement of buttons, zippers, and other hardware. Show us the damaged area, and we'll recommend the right fix.

Ask About Your Garment
Start With What You Need

Planning Your Alterations

01

Describe the Change

Tell us which garment needs attention and what feels off. You don't need to know any tailoring terms. "This sleeve feels long" or "the waist is too loose" is all we need to get started.

02

Mention Any Important Date

If the garment is for a wedding, an interview, or another big day, share the date when you contact us. Ask about timing before you make plans around the work.

03

Ask About the Details

Call or stop by to go over pricing and the alteration you have in mind. Our team will look at your garment and tell you what will work best.

Keep the Pieces You Enjoy Wearing

A Good Reason to Revisit Your Closet

Some clothes are worth more than their price tag. It might be the jacket you feel confident in, a dress tied to a special memory, or the trousers that go with almost everything.

An adjustment or repair can give that piece a second life. We focus on whatever is keeping you from wearing it, whether it's an awkward length, an unflattering shape, or a broken fastening.

Before You Bring It In

Alterations & Tailoring FAQs

Do you offer alterations and tailoring?

Yes. Our Alterations & Tailoring service covers hemming, sleeve shortening, suit and dress alterations, denim adjustments, and clothing repairs. Bring in your item and show us what you'd like changed.

Can a garment be made larger?

Often, yes, depending on how the garment is constructed and how much material is available. Bring it in, and we'll tell you what's possible.

How much will my alterations cost?

Pricing depends on the garment and the work involved. Call 864-583-8668 or stop by, describe the adjustment you need, and we'll give you pricing.

How far ahead should I arrange alterations?

Ask about current timing when you drop off. If you need the garment by a particular date, let us know right away.
