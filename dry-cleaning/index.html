When Your Clothing Needs Professional Cleaning

A care label is a good starting point, but it doesn't always tell the whole story. A stubborn mark, an unfamiliar fabric, or a piece you'd rather not risk at home are all good reasons to bring in a professional.

Bring your clothing to Bell Laundry & Cleaners and tell us what needs attention. Point out any stains and mention anything you've already tried. Those details help us give your garment the right care, so you're not left guessing what to do next.

Know What to Expect Beforehand

Dry Cleaning FAQs

How much does dry cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on the garment. Call 864-583-8668 or stop by the store, tell us about your items and any special concerns, and we'll give you pricing for the service you need.

How long does dry cleaning take?

Pickup and delivery orders typically come back within 2 to 3 business days, depending on the garment, any stains, and your location. Dropping off at the store? Ask about timing when you bring your order in, and ask about same-day service if you're short on time.

Can I get same-day dry cleaning?

Yes. We offer same-day service. Call 864-583-8668 to check the drop-off deadline and whether your items qualify.

Can you remove difficult stains?

We offer stain removal and specialty garment care. Point out the stain when you drop off, and tell us what caused it, if you know, along with anything you've already tried. We'll let you know what to expect.

Is dry cleaning the right choice for every garment?

No. Every garment has its own care needs. Bell Laundry & Cleaners offers both dry cleaning and expert wet cleaning, and our team will recommend the right one for your piece.

What should I look for in a dry cleaner in Spartanburg?

Look for professional credentials, real experience, and clear answers about pricing and timing.
