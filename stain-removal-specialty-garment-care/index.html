Tell Us What Happened

Professional Attention for Garment Stains

Maybe you know exactly when the spill happened. Maybe you only noticed the mark while getting dressed this morning. Either way, whatever you can tell us helps.

When you bring your garment in, point out the stain and let us know what caused it if you know, when you first noticed it, whether it's been washed since, and anything you've already tried on it.

Answers for Your Garment Concerns

Stain Removal and Specialty Garment Care FAQs

Can I bring an item if I don't know what caused the stain?

Yes. Point out the mark and share anything you know about when it appeared or how the garment has been cleaned.

Should I mention treatments I've already tried?

Yes, please do. Tell us which products or cleaning methods you've used, including any washing since the stain appeared.

Can you remove difficult stains?

Stain removal is one of our specialty services. Bring your garment in, show us the stain, and we'll let you know what to expect once we've had a look.

How long does specialty garment care take?

It depends on the item and the care it needs. Specialty pieces may take longer than our typical pickup and delivery turnaround of 2 to 3 business days, so ask about timing when you place your order.

What if I'm unsure which service my garment needs?

Bring it in with its care instructions, or give us a call with the details. We'll help you figure out the right service.
