For the Dress Behind the Memories

Wedding Dress Cleaning and Preservation in Spartanburg, SC

You remember exactly how it felt to wear it. The walk down the aisle, the photographs, the last dance of the night. Once the celebration is over, your dress deserves care that honors everything it was part of.

Speak With Us About Your Wedding Dress

Bell Laundry & Cleaners offers wedding dress cleaning and preservation in Spartanburg, SC. With gentle cleaning, hand finishing, and preservation packaging, we'll help you care for a dress that means so much to you.

After the Last Dance

Give Your Gown Attention Before Putting It Away

A wedding dress often comes home with more than memories. You might notice a mark along the hem, a spill from the reception, or a bead that has come loose.

It's easy to leave the dress in its garment bag while life gets back to normal. Bringing it in sooner gives you the chance to take care of those little things and decide how you'd like to keep it.

Tell us about anything you noticed during or after the wedding. A mark, a loose decoration, or something you've already tried to clean all help us give your gown the right care.

Wedding Gowns and Bridal Accessories

Your dress may be just one part of the look you want to keep. Bell Laundry & Cleaners also cares for veils, trains, and gloves.

If you'd like these pieces cared for along with your gown, bring them in together. We'll go over pricing for each item, so you know exactly what your order includes.

A Keepsake With Personal Meaning

Think About What Comes Next for Your Dress

01

Keep a Reminder of Your Wedding

You don't need plans for someone else to wear your gown to want to keep it. Its connection to your day is reason enough.

02

Consider a Future Family Keepsake

Maybe you hope to pass the dress down to a daughter, a relative, or someone special one day. Let us know when you bring it in, so we can keep that in mind.

03

Take Your Time Deciding

You may still be deciding what to do with your gown, and that's okay. Start by asking about its condition and your cleaning and preservation options. You don't need every detail figured out to get started.

Personal Details Worth Sharing

Before You Bring Us Your Wedding Dress

Bring the care label and any information from your bridal shop, if you have them. Point out anything that concerns you, like the hem, decorations, or spots where something was spilled.

Let us know what you'd like done with the dress and which accessories you're including. Ask about pricing, timing, and how to store your finished preservation box at home.

If a move or another event affects your schedule, mention it at the start. That way, we can plan around the time your gown will be away.

Your Preservation Questions Answered

Wedding Dress Cleaning and Preservation FAQs

Do you clean wedding gowns and preserve them?

Yes. We offer both. Cleaning takes care of your gown after it's been worn, and preservation prepares and packages it so you can keep it long after the wedding. Our service includes hand finishing and an archival-quality box.

Can I bring my veil or other bridal accessories?

Yes. We also clean veils, trains, and gloves. Bring them in with your gown, and we'll go over pricing for each piece.

What if my wedding dress has stains or loose decorations?

Show us the affected areas and tell us what happened, if you know. We check and repair loose threads and ornaments as part of our gown care, and we'll let you know what to expect for each stain before we begin.

Is it too late if my wedding was some time ago?

Bring the gown in and let us take a look. Tell us about the dress's condition and how it's been stored, and we'll recommend the right next step.

How much does wedding dress preservation cost?

Call 864-583-8668 for pricing on your gown and any accessories. Ask about timing when you bring it in.
