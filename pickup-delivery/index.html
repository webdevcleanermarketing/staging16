Know What to Expect

Pickup Days and Return Times

01

Service Monday Through Saturday

Our routes run Monday through Saturday. Each area has its own route days, so we'll let you know which days we serve your address when you sign up.

02

Typical Turnaround of 2 to 3 Business Days

Most pickup and delivery orders come back within 2 to 3 business days. Timing depends on the service, the garment, any stains, and your location, and specialty items may need a little longer.

Need something for an upcoming event or trip? Share the date when you schedule your order, and we'll confirm timing with you.

03

Pricing and Bags

Pickup and delivery service carries a service charge, and laundry and dry cleaning bags are available to purchase. Our team will walk you through current rates and bag options when you get started.

A Few Helpful Answers

Pickup and Delivery FAQs

Do I need to be home for pickup or delivery?

No. When you set up service, we'll go over where to leave your order so everything runs smoothly.

What days can you pick up my cleaning?

Our routes run Monday through Saturday. Your pickup day depends on your address and route.

When will my order be returned?

Most orders come back within 2 to 3 business days. The service, the garment, any stains, and your location can affect timing, and specialty items may take a little longer.

How do I schedule a pickup?

Schedule online, through our app, by phone, or by text. Call 864-583-8668 if you'd like help getting started.

Do you provide bags for my order?

Yes. Laundry and dry cleaning bags are available to purchase. We'll explain your options when you set up your first order.
