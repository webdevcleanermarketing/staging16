Helpful Answers Before Drop-Off

Comforter & Household Cleaning FAQs

Do you clean more than comforters?

Yes. We also clean blankets, bedspreads, sheets, and pillowcases. For other household items, give us a call before you bring them in.

Can I arrange pickup for my bedding?

Yes. Pickup and delivery is available based on your area and route, and it carries a service charge. Call 864-583-8668 or schedule online, through our app, or by text.

How long will my comforter take to clean?

It depends on the item and the cleaning it needs. Pickup and delivery orders typically come back within 2 to 3 business days, though larger or specialty pieces may take longer. We'll give you an expected return date when you place your order.

What should I tell you about my items?

Point out any spots or damage you've noticed, share any care instructions, and let us know if you need your items back by a certain date.

How much does household cleaning cost?

Call Bell Laundry & Cleaners at 864-583-8668 for pricing on your items and current pickup and delivery rates.
